How it works

The free kit will be delivered in plain packaging to the address you choose. This does not have to be your usual address. It can take up to 7 days to arrive but is usually quicker. Aim to do the test soon after receiving it.

Blood sample step-by-step guide

You'll need to prick your finger and fill a tube with blood. Then send it to the address on the box with pre-paid postage.

Results and timescales

The supplier will contact you within 72 hours with your result, which will show one of the following:

  • Normal: your iron levels are within the expected range.
  • Low (iron deficiency): your result will be passed to the clinician who requested this test to discuss next steps with you.

Sometimes the lab will not be able to get a clear result from a blood sample. If this happens, you'll be advised to do another self-test or visit a clinic.

Who my information is shared with

Your contact details will only be shared with the kit supplier, who is a trusted NHS partner. Your test result will be shared with the clinician who requested this test.
