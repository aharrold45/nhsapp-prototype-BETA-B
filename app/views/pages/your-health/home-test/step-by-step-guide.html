Blood sample step-by-step guide

This is an example guide to give you an idea of what's involved. Follow the instructions in the kit when it arrives.

What's in the kit

The test kit normally includes:

  • Instructions
  • Blood sample tube
  • Alcohol wipe
  • Lancets
  • Plaster
  • Transport bag
  • Security seal sticker
  • Box with pre-paid return postage
Tips before you start

These tips can help you collect your blood sample:

  • Drink a big glass of water at least 30 minutes before you do the test
  • Do the test after a shower or bath
  • Put your hand into a bowl of warm water a few minutes before doing the test
  • Swing your arms in a circular motion, or clench your hands into a fist a few times
  • Read the instructions in full before starting

Step 1: Get ready

Step 1. Wash and dry your hands with warm water and soap. Take the lid off the blood sample tube and stand it upright.

Step 2: Prepare your finger

Step 2. Decide which finger to prick and open the alcohol wipe. Rub your fingertip with the wipe and wait for it to dry.

Step 3: Prick your finger

Step 3. Remove the cap from the lancet and prick your finger. Use a tissue to dab off the first drop of blood.

Step 4: Collect the blood

Step 4. Hold your hand below waist level and gently massage the finger so blood drops fall into the tube.

Step 5: Fill the tube

Step 5. The kit instructions will tell you how much blood to collect in the tube. There are more lancets in the kit if you need to prick another finger.

Step 6: Seal and mix the tube

Step 6. Put the lid firmly back on the tube. Turn the tube upside down and back a few times to mix the blood, but do not shake the tube.

Step 7: Pack and post

Step 7. Follow the kit instructions about how to pack the blood sample. Post it to the lab using the box with pre-paid postage.
